Phurr Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

English বাংলা

The short version

  • We do not run any servers and we do not collect your personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • The optional purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Phurr is not directed to children under 13.

1. Who we are

Phurr (“the game”) is made by PicoDevs, an independent game developer in Bangladesh (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the game on Android, installed from Google Play, and when you play its web version. If anything is unclear, write to arjumansreashtho@gmail.com.

2. Information saved on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device:

  • level progress and stars, daily-puzzle history and streak, and Zen mode progress;
  • coins and hints;
  • settings such as sound, music, vibration, language, theme and accessibility options;
  • counters the game uses to space out ads (for example when the last full-screen ad was shown);
  • whether you bought “Remove ads”;
  • simple play counters such as wins, losses and hints used, which are shown only to you;
  • your own records: your best time and best score for each level and daily puzzle, and your total score. These records stay on your device and are not shared with other players.

Our code never sends this information off your device: we have no server to send it to, and the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game’s storage in your phone’s settings or by uninstalling the game.

Device backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the game’s saved data in your device backup to your Google Account. Google manages these backups under your account settings; we cannot access them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Reward videos are always optional: you choose to watch one for a hint, an extra heart or double coins. Short full-screen ads may appear between levels, never while you are playing a level, unless you bought “Remove ads”.

Google describes the data that the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares automatically for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention (Google’s data disclosure):

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this information as described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. AdMob gives us only aggregated reports, such as the number of ads shown and earnings; these do not tell us who you are.

Your ad choices

  • Consent in Europe. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent message, made with AdMob’s Privacy & messaging tool, before it requests ads. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which use less data.
  • Change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options inside the game. The button opens Google’s form wherever a choice applies; elsewhere it tells you that no options are needed on your device.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (on many phones: Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads). After you delete it, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an ID.
  • Google’s ad settings. You can see and control how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

The game offers one optional, one-time purchase: “Remove ads”. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card number or other payment details. Google Play tells the game on your device that the purchase is complete (the product, a purchase token and its status), so the game can turn off the ads between levels and let you restore the purchase later. This stays on your device. Google may make limited order information available to us in the Google Play Console so we can handle the order, refunds and support requests, as described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Permissions

The Android app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. It uses only “normal” permissions that Android grants without a prompt: internet and network state (for ads and purchases), the advertising ID (used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK), Google Play billing, and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. The Google libraries for ads and billing also add a few other “normal” technical permissions automatically, for example to check whether the device is online or to keep ads working after an update. None of them gives access to your personal data, and none needs your approval.

6. Analytics and crash reports

The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. Google Play gives developers aggregated statistics and technical reports, such as install numbers, ratings, and crash or “app not responding” reports, collected from users who have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These reports do not identify you to us.

7. Web version

If you play the web version, your progress is kept in your browser’s local storage on your device. The web version shows no real ads (reward videos are simulated) and offers no purchases. The website host may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

8. Children

Phurr is made for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; in fact, as described above, we do not collect personal information from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please contact us.

9. Keeping and deleting data

We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing on our side to keep or delete. Data on your device stays until you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it. Google keeps the data it collects according to its own policies; you can manage it with the controls in section 3.

10. Security

Google states that the data the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects is encrypted in transit using TLS. Data saved on your device is protected by your device’s own security, such as your screen lock.

11. Where data is processed and your rights

Google may process data in countries other than yours. Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you, the most useful controls are the in-game Privacy options, your phone’s ad settings and Google’s tools. You can always write to us with a question or request.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if we add analytics or online features, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released, and update the game’s Data safety information on Google Play.
