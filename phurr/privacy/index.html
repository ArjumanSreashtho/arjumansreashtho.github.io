Phurr Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

English বাংলা

The short version

  • Phurr collects no data. We run no servers, and there are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The Android app has no internet permission: it cannot send anything off your device.
  • Phurr is not directed to children under 13.

1. Who we are

Phurr (“the game”) is made by PicoDevs, an independent game developer in Bangladesh (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the game on Android, installed from Google Play, and when you play its web version. If anything is unclear, write to support@picodevs.com.

2. Information saved on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device:

  • level progress and stars, daily-puzzle history and streak, and Zen mode progress;
  • coins and hints;
  • settings such as sound, music, vibration, language, theme and accessibility options;
  • your best times and scores (records);
  • simple play counters such as wins, losses and hints used, which are shown only to you.

This information never leaves your device through the game: we have no server to send it to, and the game contains no code that sends it anywhere. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game’s storage in your phone’s settings or by uninstalling the game.

Device backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the game’s saved data in your device backup to your Google Account. Google manages these backups under your account settings; we cannot access them.

3. No ads and no tracking

The game shows no ads of any kind and contains no advertising software. It does not use the Android advertising ID, does not build a profile of you and does not track you across apps or websites. Hints and an extra heart are paid with coins that you earn by playing.

4. Purchases

There are none. The game has no in-app purchases and does not include Google Play’s billing library, so it never handles payment information.

5. Permissions

The Android app asks for no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it has no internet permission. Its only permission is vibration, for haptic feedback, which Android grants without a prompt and which you can turn off in the game’s Settings.

6. Analytics and crash reports

The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. Google Play gives developers aggregated statistics and technical reports, such as install numbers, ratings, and crash or “app not responding” reports, collected by Google from users who have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These reports do not identify you to us.

7. Third-party software

The game is built with Capacitor and a few of its official plugins (for the back button, vibration, saving progress on the device, the launch screen and the status bar), together with Android’s own libraries. None of them collect or send data. The game’s fonts are files inside the app, so no font is loaded from the internet.

8. Web version

If you play the web version, your progress is kept in your browser’s local storage on your device. The web version has no ads, no purchases and no analytics. The website host may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

9. Children

Phurr is made for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please contact us.

10. Keeping and deleting data

We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing on our side to keep or delete. Data on your device stays until you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it.

11. Security

Because nothing is sent off your device, no data is exposed in transit. Data saved on your device is protected by your device’s own security, such as your screen lock.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. We hold no personal data about you; the data on your device is fully under your control as described in section 2. You can always write to us with a question or request.

13. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if we add ads, analytics or online features, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released, and update the game’s Data safety information on Google Play.
