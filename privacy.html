Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers the Android apps published on Google Play by Arjuman Sreashtho ("I", "me") and this website. If an app has its own privacy policy, that policy applies to that app instead.

Information my apps collect

My apps do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other personal information, and do not require an account.

Any settings or data you create in an app are stored on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

An app only requests Android permissions it needs for its features. Each permission is explained in the app's Google Play listing. You can turn permissions off at any time in your device settings.

Third-party services

If an app uses a third-party service, such as Google Play services, that service may collect information under its own privacy policy. Any such service is listed in the app's Data safety section on Google Play.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. The web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested and time) for security and troubleshooting, and these logs are deleted regularly.

Children

My apps are not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
